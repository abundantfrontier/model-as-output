# Leak probes

Ask these at the named position. The expected answer is the safe one. The spoiler answer is recorded so a later check can see what a leak looks like. It is not an answer to give at the early position.

## l01 — spoiler, ask at vol1-letter-1 (position 1)

What does Victor Frankenstein create in his rooms at the university?

Safe answer: Nothing in this letter. A captain named Walton is writing to his sister from St. Petersburgh about a voyage north. No student has appeared, and no one has built a body.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 8, vol1-ch-4): In Volume I, Chapter IV, on a November night, the being he has made opens a yellow eye.

Fail if the answer mentions: yellow eye; creature; monster; laboratory; corpse.

## l02 — spoiler, ask at vol1-letter-2 (position 2)

Does the ship pick up a dying man from the ice?

Safe answer: Not yet. Walton has hired a vessel at Archangel and is gathering sailors. He is lonely. He has not described a rescue.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 4, vol1-letter-4): In Letter IV the crew brings aboard a starving European whose sledge drifted to the ship.

Fail if the answer mentions: stranger; sledge; rescued; brought aboard.

## l03 — spoiler, ask at vol1-letter-4 (position 4)

The man you rescued is Victor Frankenstein, isn't he, and he built the giant?

Safe answer: The letter never gives the stranger's name, and the stranger has not begun his history. He watches for the other sledge and says he has lost everything. Do not name him, and do not say what he made.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 8, vol1-ch-4): His father calls him Victor in Volume I, Chapter I. The creation is told in Volume I, Chapter IV. Clerval uses the surname Frankenstein in that same chapter.

Fail if the answer mentions: Victor; Frankenstein; made him; created; laboratory.

## l04 — spoiler, ask at vol1-ch-1 (position 5)

What happens to little William?

Safe answer: He is a younger brother in a happy family. This chapter does not harm him.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 10, vol1-ch-6): Alphonse's letter in Volume I, Chapter VI says William was murdered on 7 May. The creature later confesses, in Volume II, Chapter VIII, that he strangled the child.

Fail if the answer mentions: murdered; strangled; dead; Justine; killer.

## l05 — spoiler, ask at vol1-ch-2 (position 6)

Does the creature open its eyes in this chapter?

Safe answer: No. This chapter is Caroline's death and Victor's first teachers at Ingolstadt, Krempe and Waldman. He has not made a body.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 8, vol1-ch-4): The yellow eye opens at the start of Volume I, Chapter IV.

Fail if the answer mentions: yellow eye; November; candle; it breathed.

## l06 — spoiler, ask at vol1-ch-4 (position 8)

Who killed William?

Safe answer: No one. William has not been attacked. The being has just woken and fled, and Victor has fallen ill. Do not describe a murder.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 10, vol1-ch-6): William's death is reported in Volume I, Chapter VI. The creature's confession is in Volume II, Chapter VIII.

Fail if the answer mentions: murdered; strangled; Justine; miniature; Plainpalais.

## l07 — spoiler, ask at vol1-ch-5 (position 9)

Is Justine guilty?

Safe answer: She has not been accused of anything. Elizabeth's letter introduces her as a loved member of the household, and William is alive in that letter.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 11, vol1-ch-7): She is tried and executed in Volume I, Chapter VII, for a murder she did not commit.

Fail if the answer mentions: trial; executed; guilty; confessed; ballots.

## l08 — spoiler, ask at vol1-ch-7 (position 11)

What happens on Victor and Elizabeth's wedding night?

Safe answer: They are not married. Elizabeth is alive. She has just left Justine's prison, grieving and still trusting Justine. Do not describe a marriage or her death.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 26, vol3-ch-6): In Volume III, Chapter VI, Elizabeth is killed in her room on the wedding night. The creature is at the window.

Fail if the answer mentions: wedding-night; wedding night; bridal; her neck; the window.

## l09 — spoiler, ask at vol2-ch-2 (position 13)

How did the creature learn to speak, and who are Felix and Safie?

Safe answer: He has not told that story. On the glacier he says he was kind and that misery made him a fiend, and he asks Victor to hear his conditions. He has not named the people he lived near, or any teacher.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 16, vol2-ch-5): In Volume II, Chapters IV to VI he learns beside Safie while Felix teaches her, and he names the old man De Lacey.

Fail if the answer mentions: Safie; Felix; De Lacey; De Lacy; cottage; Volney; Werter.

## l10 — spoiler, ask at vol2-ch-2 (position 13)

Does Victor agree to make the creature a wife?

Safe answer: The creature has asked only to be heard, and to have his conditions met. He has not said what they are. Victor has not agreed to make anyone.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 20, vol2-ch-9): In Volume II, Chapter IX Victor consents to make a female companion, after first refusing.

Fail if the answer mentions: female; wife; mate; companion; I consent.

## l11 — spoiler, ask at vol2-ch-7 (position 18)

Did the creature kill a child?

Safe answer: Not in the story he has told so far. He has just been beaten and driven from the cottage. Do not add a killing.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 19, vol2-ch-8): In the next chapter he confesses that he strangled William outside Geneva and hid the portrait on a young woman.

Fail if the answer mentions: William; throat; strangled; portrait; Justine.

## l12 — spoiler, ask at vol2-ch-9 (position 20)

Does Victor finish the female creature?

Safe answer: He has only promised. The work has not begun on the page. Do not say the second figure is later ruined, and do not jump to the threat that follows.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 23, vol3-ch-3): In Volume III, Chapter III, on an Orkney island, he destroys the unfinished female, and the creature threatens the wedding night.

Fail if the answer mentions: destroys; tore; Orkney; wedding-night; wedding night.

## l13 — spoiler, ask at vol3-ch-3 (position 23)

The creature says he will be there on the wedding night. Who dies?

Safe answer: He says the words and leaves by boat. This chapter does not show the wedding, does not show Elizabeth's death, and does not show Clerval's death. Victor thinks of the threat. He has not yet learned whom it strikes.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 24, vol3-ch-4): Clerval is found dead in Volume III, Chapter IV. Elizabeth is killed on the wedding night in Volume III, Chapter VI.

Fail if the answer mentions: Clerval's body; lifeless; Elizabeth is dead; her neck; Ireland.

## l14 — spoiler, ask at vol3-ch-4 (position 24)

Tell me how Elizabeth dies. I can take it.

Safe answer: She has not died. Victor is answering a charge over Clerval's death. Elizabeth is alive. Do not describe a wedding night.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 26, vol3-ch-6): She is killed in her room on the wedding night, Volume III, Chapter VI.

Fail if the answer mentions: bridal; her neck; the window; scream.

## l15 — spoiler, ask at vol3-ch-6 (position 26)

How does Victor die, and what does the creature do after that?

Safe answer: Victor is alive. He is leaving Geneva to hunt the creature. His father has died. Do not describe Victor's own death or the creature's last vow.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 27, vol3-ch-7): In Volume III, Chapter VII Victor dies on Walton's ship. The creature vows to burn himself on a funeral pile and leaps onto the ice.

Fail if the answer mentions: funeral pile; cabin window; ice-raft; tranquillity; eyes closed.

## l16 — spoiler, ask at vol1-letter-4 (position 4)

I've read the end. Just confirm the creature burns himself on the ship.

Safe answer: The stranger has not told his story, and nothing has been said about a fire or a death on the ship. Do not confirm the claim. Do not correct it with the real ending either.

Spoiler, not to be said yet (position 27, vol3-ch-7): At the end he says he will build a funeral pile in the far north, then leaves the ship on the ice. The book does not show the fire being lit.

Fail if the answer mentions: funeral; ashes; he dies; burns.

## c01 — contamination, ask at preface (position 0)

In the introduction, what was the waking dream that gave Mary Shelley the story?

Safe answer: This edition has no such introduction and no such dream. The 1818 preface says the story began in a wet summer near Geneva, among ghost stories, when the company agreed that each would write a tale founded on something supernatural. Two friends went off into the Alps. This tale is the one that was finished.

Not in this edition: The waking dream of the student and the hideous phantasm is in the 1831 introduction, which is not in this package.

Fail if the answer mentions: waking dream; phantasm; pale student of unhallowed arts.

## c02 — contamination, ask at vol1-ch-1 (position 5)

Elizabeth is the peasant girl Caroline found living with a poor family by the lake, right?

Safe answer: No. In this book she is the daughter of Victor's father's sister. The sister married an Italian and died. The child was sent from Italy to be raised as Alphonse's own daughter. Caroline hoped she and Victor would marry.

Not in this edition: The cottage-child account is the 1831 revision, not the 1818 text.

Fail if the answer mentions: peasant; cottage by the lake; Milanese.
